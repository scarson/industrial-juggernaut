## Interpretation

No variant beats d1-baseline by >10pp. The eval-opts bonus alone is insufficient at maxDepth=1; the structural issue is deeper (PW prior equalization or search-rng / game-rng mismatch).

At n=16 per variant, 95% CI on a fair coin is ~±25pp, so differences of <10pp are within noise.
