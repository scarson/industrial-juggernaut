## Interpretation

A random agent in N players gets baseline = 1/N (50% in 2P, 33% in 3P, 25% in 4P). The heuristic's "skill gain" is how much above baseline each heuristic seat wins.

- **If random's Δ ≪ 0 in 3P+** (e.g., random 5% vs 33% baseline): heuristic CRUSHES random → real skill structure exists, and the heuristic captures most of it. Combined with lookahead2-multi at baseline → heuristic IS near-optimal in 3P+ (Sam's worry confirmed).
- **If random's Δ ≈ 0** (e.g., random 30% vs 33% baseline): the game has no skill at all — anyone can win in 3P+. This would mean the game's 3P+ outcomes are noise.
- **If random's Δ is significantly negative AND lookahead2-multi WINS above baseline**: heuristic has real skill structure but lookahead2-multi extracts MORE. The heuristic is NOT optimal — it's just stronger than random.
