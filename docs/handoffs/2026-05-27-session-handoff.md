### History — why this looked like a crossroads (S5, now superseded)
> ⚠️ Everything below was the S5→calibration reasoning **before** the MCTS revalidation. The "calibrated re-run / threshold reconsideration / redesign" paths and the "balanceable" framing are SUPERSEDED by the agent-relative finding above and analysis §0.1. **What S5 actually found (the nuance that matters):**
- **0/64 configs pass all 7 criteria.** The setup-decided degeneracy is *gone* almost everywhere — `setupDecidedFraction = 0` for 56/64 cells (the base-placement fix worked). The two binding failure modes are now: **`medianTurns` too short** (games end turn 1–2 across most of the grid — the radius-N base still blankets enough iron to end fast) and **`seatWinBias > 0.20`** (fails in nearly every cell).
- **Confound #1 — seatBias is at its statistical noise floor.** seatBias values quantize to 0.167 / 0.233 / 0.333 / 0.417 / 0.5 — i.e. multiples of ~1/6 of a ~30-game per-seat-count bucket (150 games ÷ the 2–6P rotation). At ~30 games/seat-count the per-seat win-rate CI is ≈ ±0.18, so a 0.20 cap is *barely above noise*; many "seatBias FAIL"s are plausibly sampling artifacts, not real first-mover advantage. **Until games/config is raised enough to separate signal from noise, seatBias failures are not trustworthy.**
- **Confound #2 — two configs miss by exactly ONE criterion**, and each missed criterion is one of the two soft ones:
  - `boardSize=96, radius=2, ironCount=12, victoryThreshold=12`: passes 6/7 (median 3, setupDecided 0, ironVic 0.78, leadVol 0.31) — **fails only seatBias 0.233** (likely noise per confound #1).
  - `boardSize=96, radius=2, ironCount=16, victoryThreshold=12`: passes 6/7 — seatBias 0.167 **passes** — **fails only medianTurns 2** (one turn under the min-3 "has depth" band).
- **OFAT around the near-miss (directional, weak-agent caveat):** `victoryThreshold` is the strongest length lever (10→median 2, 12→3, 14→18 but ironVic collapses to 0 — i.e. 14 is too high to reach). `killBounty` full→none drops ironVic 0.78→0.68 (mild). `autoWinAt6` on/off barely moves anything. `attackRange` 5 vs 6 mildly affects ironVic.

**Why this is a crossroads, not an autonomous fix.** The two binding criteria are *definitional game-design judgments*, not bugs: (a) is "median 2 turns" actually too short, or is the min-3 "has depth" band miscalibrated? (b) is the 0.20 seatBias cap meaningful at 150 games, or must games/config rise (≈600+ to halve the CI) before seatBias is even measurable? Both questions are Sam's to answer. Three honest paths, in increasing cost:
1. **Calibrated re-run (cheapest, recommended first):** raise games/config (≈600+) to pull seatBias out of the noise floor, and narrow the grid around the `b96/r2/vt12` near-misses (the only region producing median-3 multi-turn games with low setupDecided). If a config then passes all 7, the game *is* balanceable and we have it. **This must happen before any "unbalanceable" conclusion — the current run cannot distinguish "no healthy config" from "under-powered measurement."** (Do the queued progress-logging FIRST so the longer run is observable.)
2. **Threshold reconsideration (Sam-gated):** if Sam judges "median 2 turns" acceptable or the seatBias cap too strict at achievable sample sizes, the near-miss `b96/r2/iron16/vt12` is already a defensible balanced baseline. This is loosening a *definition*, not loosening a gate to manufacture a pass — but it's Sam's call, not the agent's.
3. **Redesign-level finding (only if #1 fails):** if a calibrated, narrowed re-run still finds nothing, *that* is the genuine "the geometry/economy can't produce balanced multi-turn games" verdict — surface it to Sam as a rules-level redesign question (the radius-N base blanketing iron is the mechanism; it ends games before strategy develops).

**Prior context (still true):** the base-placement fix made the game *playable* but games are decided fast (a radius-5 base on a ~93-hex board blankets ≥10 of 14 iron; pre-fix, 48/200 games won AT SETUP). No single hand-picked knob fixed it (validated) — which is why the sweep exists. S5 shows the *setup* degeneracy is fixed but a *turn-1-end* degeneracy remains in most of the grid.
